## 🎯 Project Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze Decathlon-style retail transaction data and convert it into meaningful business insights through an interactive Excel dashboard.

The dashboard focuses on answering key business questions:

* How are overall sales and orders performing?
* Which sports generate the highest sales?
* Which product categories contribute the most revenue?
* How large is the customer base?
* What is the average order value?
* How does customer retention change over time?
* How does sales contribution vary by gender?
* Which months and periods show stronger sales performance?

---

## 🏢 Business Context

For a large sporting-goods retailer, monitoring sales and customer behavior across sports, product categories, time periods, and customer segments is important for making better commercial decisions.

This dashboard provides a centralized view of these metrics and helps decision-makers identify:

* High-performing sports
* Revenue-driving product categories
* Monthly sales trends
* Customer retention patterns
* Order behavior
* Gender-based sales contribution
* Opportunities for improving customer engagement

The monthly sales trend visual helps identify:
